Karaoke King Audit

A local tool that helps the owner of Karaoke King review channel information and deliver completed karaoke videos.

What the tool does

  • Reads the authorized channel’s videos, playlists and upload status.
  • Reads channel analytics when the separate read-only analytics connection is authorized.
  • Uploads owner-approved videos and thumbnails, then verifies processing and publication status.

Google authorization is completed through Google’s consent screen. The application runs in the owner’s private local workflow; this information site does not offer public sign-in or access to the channel.

Clear, limited access

Access is used for the owner’s channel workflow. Credentials, downloaded song sources, production media and private operational records are not published on this site or in its repository.

Read how Google data is used and retained and the terms of use.

Contact

Questions about the tool or these policies can be raised through the project’s contact page. Do not post credentials, tokens or private channel information in a public issue.
