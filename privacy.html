Data practices

Privacy policy

Karaoke King Audit is a personal channel-management tool used by the channel owner and authorized operators personally known to the owner.

Effective November 2, 2026

Information accessed

When the owner grants permission, the tool accesses channel and video metadata, playlists, upload and processing status, and, through a separately authorized read-only connection, channel analytics. The publishing connection requests YouTube read-only and upload access. The analytics connection requests YouTube read-only and YouTube Analytics read-only access. This policy does not grant any additional permission.

How information is used

Information is used to audit the authorized channel, avoid duplicate uploads, prepare accurate channel assets, upload completed owner-authorized videos and thumbnails, and verify their processing and publication status. Channel analytics support the owner’s content planning and performance review.

The tool does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or provide it to other app users. It does not operate a general-purpose model-training pipeline.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are kept in private local credential storage outside the production repository. Operational recipes, identity checks, QA reports, review samples and upload/deletion receipts may be retained in the owner’s local workspace. An owner-controlled private dashboard may contain a sanitized view of production status.

After exact upload identity and successful YouTube processing are verified, job-owned downloaded sources, generated audio and output videos are automatically retired under the owner’s cleanup policy. Original archives, supplied or pinned sources, failed or uncertain uploads and compact evidence are handled separately and may remain. Removing local media does not delete the YouTube upload.

Services and sharing

Google and YouTube process requests needed for the authorized API operations. Limited operational information may be processed through the owner’s selected coding assistant, automation services and private dashboard as part of the owner-directed workflow. Credentials are excluded from public pages, public repositories and dashboard snapshots. Information is not shared for unrelated purposes; disclosure may also occur if legally required.

This static information site has no application sign-in, forms or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary request logs under the GitHub privacy statement.

Google API policy

Karaoke King Audit’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not itself delete existing local records or videos on YouTube. The owner can remove local credentials and operational records or request assistance through the project’s contact page. Private details should not be posted publicly.

Updates and contact

This policy will be updated when the tool’s data practices change. Questions or requests can be raised through the project contact page linked above.
